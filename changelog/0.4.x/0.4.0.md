# 0.4.0 — 2026-11-22

## Added

- **`ref://timezones/{region}/{subregion}/{city}`** returns `unencoded_slash` with the encoded retry URI for a three-segment IANA ID such as `America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires`. ([#54](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/54))

## Changed

- **Breaking: timezone queries require whole names.** Replace prefixes such as `Auckl` or `Reykj` with a complete city or location name or an exact IANA ID. Replace signed UTC/GMT shorthand, including `GMT+0` and `GMT-0` in conversion, with an exact ID such as `UTC` or `Etc/GMT-5`. ([#48](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/48))
- **Breaking: ambiguous city names** such as Kingston, Victoria, Valencia, Sydney, Saint-Pierre, and Tripoli require an exact IANA ID in either `ref_timezone_convert` operand. `ref_timezone_lookup` returns all candidates, and the conversion error includes their IDs. ([#47](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/47))
- **Breaking: atomic-number strings** in `ref_element_lookup` (`auto` and `number`) and `ref://elements/{number}` must consist of ASCII digits. Replace `+6`, padded whitespace, `6.0`, and `6e0` with `6`. Leading zeros and once-encoded digits in resource URIs are supported. ([#52](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/52))
- **`ref_unit_convert`** documents all 23 supported measures and includes a working spelling for each in unknown-unit guidance. ([#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/50))
- **Caller text** in error messages, notices, and filters shows non-displayable characters as Unicode escapes. Structured identifiers and filter values retain the original text. ([#51](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/51))
- **Tool failures** for 14 modeled caller outcomes, including lookup misses and invalid conversion inputs, log at `notice`. ([#53](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/53))
- **`ref_unicode_lookup`** in `auto` mode inspects literal notation lists and mixed bare hex such as `200B` as text and advises how to request the named code points. ([#56](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/56))

## Fixed

- **Timezone lookup and conversion** use the historical rules of each matching tzdb member, such as `America/Rosario` for Rosario. ([#46](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/46))
- **Named timezone lookup and reads** accept runtime-supported links like `US/Eastern` and fixed-offset zones like `Etc/GMT+5`. Singapore and Jamaica still resolve to their catalog records. The resource reason `no_timezone_data` is retired. ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/49))
- **Timezone lookup** rejects impossible ISO calendar dates in `at` with `invalid_at`, reports the daylight abbreviation at the queried instant and the standard abbreviation from the standard season, and renders every major city in the text result.
- **Element lookup and reads** reject malformed atomic numbers such as `74.9`, `1e2`, and `6abc` that previously returned records. ([#52](https://github.com/cyanheads/reference-data-mcp-server/issues/52))
